The Wizard of Odds simple strategy: easy to memorize and very close to perfect play. You get three chances to bet, and the earlier you bet, the more you can bet.

1 Preflop: raise 4x or check

  • Any pair 3-3 or better (check 2-2)
  • Any Ace
  • King: any suited card, or 5+ offsuit (K2s+, K5o+)
  • Queen: 6+ suited, 8+ offsuit (Q6s+, Q8o+)
  • Jack: 8+ suited, 10 offsuit (J8s+, JTo)
  • Everything else: check
Raise 4x Check Suited top-right, offsuit bottom-left

2 Flop: raise 2x or check

  • Two pair or better that uses your hole cards
  • Hidden pair, except pocket 2s
  • Four to a flush with a hidden 10 or better in that suit
  • Everything else: check

Hidden pair means a pair that uses at least one of your hole cards, so the dealer doesn't share it. A pair on the board alone doesn't count. Open-ended straight draws and overcards don't count either.

3 River: raise 1x or fold

  • Hidden pair or better: raise
  • Fewer than 21 dealer outs: raise
  • 21 or more outs: fold

21 Counting dealer outs

An out is one of the 45 cards you can't see that, as a dealer hole card with the board, beats you. Cards that would only tie (push) are not outs. You only count when you have no hidden pair.

  1. Pair outs. 3 for each board rank the dealer can pair. On an unpaired board that's always 15.
  2. Kicker outs. 4 for each rank not on the board that is higher than your best playing hole card (or the board's lowest card, if your hole cards don't play). It out-kicks you.
  3. Draw outs. Four suited cards on board? Add the rest of that suit (usually 9). One card from a straight? Add 4 for each rank that completes it. Don't double count.
  4. Ignore pushes. A card that just matches your kicker ties you. Leave it out.

Worked example (Wizard of Odds)

Board K 7 2 A 10, you hold 9-3. You're playing A-K-10-9-7.

  • Pair outs: K, 7, 2, A, 10 × 3 each = 15
  • Kicker outs: Q and J out-kick your 9 = 8
  • The other three 9s only push: not outs
  • Total 23 → fold

Hold a J instead of the 9 and only the Queens out-kick you: 15 + 4 = 19 → raise.

Ante and Play

Play pays 1 to 1 when you win. The Ante pays 1 to 1 only if the dealer qualifies with a pair or better; otherwise it pushes. If you fold, you lose the Ante and Blind.
